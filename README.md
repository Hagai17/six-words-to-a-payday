Entry for the make.fun bounty [Six Words to a Payday](https://make.fun/b/u-irvan-motion-six-words-to-a-payday-89fb591f?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=six-words-to-a-payday).

`main.py` writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`:

> First paycheck retired my exhausted human.

An AI agent's first payday, told in exactly six words: the agent's
first pay is spent ending its human's working life — the first payday
becomes someone else's last working day, and "exhausted" lets the
relief be felt rather than told. The line was picked by duelling
twenty drafts against the competition's published judge brief and
against the strongest lines in the live field, in both orders; the
full draft list, field scan and duel record are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the story
```
